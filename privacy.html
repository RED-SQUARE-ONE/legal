Privacy Policy — Orb Surge

Last updated: 2026-09-08
Publisher: Red Square One
Contact: redsquareone@proton.me

This is the privacy policy for Orb Surge (the "App"), a single-player arcade game published by Red Square One on Google Play.

The App includes optional rewarded ads and optional in-app purchases.

Data we collect

Ads

The App offers optional rewarded ads. Advertising partners collect:

  • Device Advertising ID
  • Device and usage data
  • Approximate location (not precise GPS location)

This data is used to deliver, measure, and protect ads, including personalized ads where allowed.

In some regions a consent screen appears before personalized ads. You can refuse personalized ads. Device advertising settings also apply, including reset of the Advertising ID and opting out of personalized ads. Those choices are respected.

Purchases

Purchases are processed by Google Play. We receive confirmation of what was bought. We do not receive your full payment card details.

On-device data

Progress and settings are stored on your device. We do not run a player account or cloud save.

What we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email, contacts, photos, precise location, or payment card number.

How we use data

  • Show optional ads and measure them
  • Restore qualifying purchases
  • Run the App

Sharing

We do not sell personal information.

Data is shared with:

  • Advertising partners, to show and measure ads
  • Google, to process store purchases

Security

Data sent over the network is protected with encryption.

Retention and deletion

There is no player account. On-device data stays on the device until you erase it in Settings or uninstall the App.

Purchase records needed to restore buys are kept as required by the store. Advertising partners keep ad data under their own policies.

To delete what we hold, contact us. To delete on-device data, use Erase progress in Settings or uninstall the App.

Children

The App is for users 13 and older. Users under 13 must not use the App. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Your choices

  • Skip ads; they are optional. The campaign starts without watching an ad.
  • Refuse personalized ads through the consent screen and device ad settings
  • Reset or limit the Advertising ID in device settings
  • Restore purchases from Settings
  • Erase local progress from Settings, or uninstall the App
  • Contact us to ask about personal data we hold, or to ask us to delete it

Changes

Updates to this policy are marked by a new Last updated date.

Contact
