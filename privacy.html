Privacy Policy — Orb Surge

Last updated: 2026-09-08
Publisher: Red Square One
Contact: redsquareone@proton.me

Orb Surge ("the App") is a single-player arcade game published by Red Square One.

This policy describes data handling if the release build includes AppLovin MAX rewarded ads and Google Play Billing. If ads are removed from a build, this policy must be updated so it matches that binary.

Data we collect

Rewarded ads (AppLovin MAX)

The App may show optional rewarded ads via AppLovin MAX. AppLovin and its advertising partners may collect:

  • Device identifiers (Advertising ID / AAID; IDFA on iOS only with permission)
  • IP address and approximate location (not GPS)
  • Device and app interaction data for ad delivery, measurement, and fraud prevention

The App does not include Google AdMob as a direct SDK. In MAX, Google AdMob / Google bidding should stay disabled unless this policy and the Play Data Safety form are updated.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, a consent form (CMP / IAB TCF v2) is shown before personalized ads. You can refuse personalized ads. You can reopen privacy choices from Settings when that control is available.

On iOS, App Tracking Transparency may also appear. You may decline.

AppLovin privacy policy: https://www.applovin.com/privacy/

Purchases

Google Play (and Apple on iOS, if shipped) process payments. We receive purchase confirmation (product ID and status). We do not receive your full payment card details.

Local save data

Progress, sparks, settings, and unlocks are stored on your device only. We do not operate a game account server. There is no cloud save.

Network time check

The daily reward uses an HTTP request to read a trusted Date header (not GPS). This is to limit clock cheating. No account is created.

What we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email, contacts, photos, precise location, or payment card number. We do not run analytics or crash-reporting SDKs in the current App.

How we use data

  • Deliver optional rewarded ads and measure ad performance (ad partners)
  • Restore non-consumable purchases (premium orbs, world unlocks)
  • Run the daily reward fairly

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Ad partners process data under their own policies when ads are shown. Google processes purchase data under Google Play terms.

Children

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children. Do not use the App if you are under 13.

Your choices

  • Skip rewarded ads; they are optional
  • Decline personalized ads via the consent form / device ad settings / ATT
  • Restore purchases from Settings (this restores Play purchases, not a user account)
  • Erase local progress from Settings, or uninstall the App to delete local save data

Changes

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date will change.
