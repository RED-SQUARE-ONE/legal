Privacy Policy — Orb Surge

Last updated: 2026-09-08
Publisher: Red Square One
Contact: redsquareone@proton.me

Orb Surge (the "App") is a single-player arcade game published by Red Square One on Google Play.

The App includes optional rewarded ads through AppLovin MAX and in-app purchases through Google Play Billing.

Data we collect

Rewarded ads (AppLovin MAX)

The App offers optional rewarded ads through AppLovin MAX. AppLovin and its advertising partners collect:

  • Device identifiers (Advertising ID / AAID)
  • IP address and approximate location (not GPS)
  • Device and app interaction data for ad delivery, measurement, and fraud prevention

The App does not include Google AdMob. Google AdMob bidding is disabled in MAX.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, a consent form (CMP / IAB TCF v2) appears before personalized ads. You can refuse personalized ads. Device advertising settings also apply.

AppLovin privacy policy: https://www.applovin.com/privacy/

Purchases

Google Play processes payments. We receive purchase confirmation (product ID and status). We do not receive your full payment card details.

Local save data

Progress, sparks, settings, and unlocks are stored on your device only. We do not operate a game account server. There is no cloud save.

Network time check

The daily reward uses an HTTP request to read a trusted Date header (not GPS). This limits clock cheating. No account is created.

What we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email, contacts, photos, precise location, or payment card number. We do not run analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.

How we use data

  • Deliver optional rewarded ads and measure ad performance (ad partners)
  • Restore non-consumable purchases (premium orbs, world unlocks)
  • Run the daily reward fairly

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Ad partners process data under their own policies when ads are shown. Google processes purchase data under Google Play terms.

Children

The App is for users 13 and older. Users under 13 must not use the App. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Your choices

  • Skip rewarded ads; they are optional. The campaign starts without watching an ad.
  • Refuse personalized ads through the consent form and device ad settings
  • Restore purchases from Settings (this restores Play purchases, not a user account)
  • Erase local progress from Settings, or uninstall the App to delete local save data

Changes

Updates to this policy are marked by a new Last updated date.
