Privacy Policy — Orb Surge

Last updated: 2026-09-08
Publisher: Red Square One
Contact: redsquareone@proton.me

Orb Surge (the "App") is a single-player arcade game published by Red Square One on Google Play.

The App includes optional rewarded ads and optional in-app purchases.

Data we collect

Ads

The App offers optional rewarded ads. Advertising partners collect device and usage data to deliver, measure, and protect those ads.

In some regions a consent screen appears before personalized ads. You can refuse personalized ads. Device advertising settings also apply.

Purchases

Purchases are processed by Google Play. We receive confirmation of what was bought. We do not receive your full payment card details.

On-device data

Progress and settings are stored on your device. We do not run a player account or cloud save.

What we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email, contacts, photos, precise location, or payment card number.

How we use data

  • Show optional ads
  • Restore qualifying purchases
  • Run the App

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Advertising partners and Google handle data under their own policies for ads and store purchases.

Children

The App is for users 13 and older. Users under 13 must not use the App. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Your choices

  • Skip ads; they are optional. The campaign starts without watching an ad.
  • Refuse personalized ads through the consent screen and device ad settings
  • Restore purchases from Settings
  • Erase local progress from Settings, or uninstall the App to delete local save data

Changes

Updates to this policy are marked by a new Last updated date.
